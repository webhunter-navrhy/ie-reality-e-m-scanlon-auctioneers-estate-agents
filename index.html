New to the market this spacious detached three bedroom cottage set on an elevated C.1 acre site, conveniently located just 1km from the N4 at Castlebaldwin. In need of complete refurbishment and modernisation, this property presents an excellent opportunity for renovation and may qualify for the Derelict Property Refurbishment Grant (subject to eligibility criteria). Current accommodation comprises entrance porch, open-plan living/dining room, three bedrooms, bathroom and rear kitchen. Externally, the property benefits from a greenhouse, polytunnel and timber outbuilding suitable for a home office, studio or workshop. Services include a septic tank and water connection onsite, however a new electricity connection will be required. A unique opportunity to create a charming countryside home in a highly accessible location. Viewing by appointment. Full details on request
